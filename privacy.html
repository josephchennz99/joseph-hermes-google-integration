Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Scope

Joseph’s Hermes Google Integration is a private, single-user application used only by Joseph Chen to connect his personal Hermes assistant to his own Google account. It is not offered to the public.

2. Google data accessed

When Joseph authorises and requests a task, the integration may access:

  • Gmail message metadata and content needed to search, read, organise, or prepare saved drafts. The integration is not permitted to send email.
  • Google Calendar events needed to read, create, update, reschedule, or remove events at Joseph’s direction.
  • Google Drive files and metadata needed to read, create, rename, move, or organise files at Joseph’s direction. The integration is not permitted to permanently delete Drive files.
  • Basic Google account identity information required for authentication and account verification.

3. How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the specific user-facing task requested by Joseph, maintain the authorised integration, prevent duplicate actions, and verify results. It is not used for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated purposes.

4. Processing and disclosure

Authorised data is processed within Joseph’s private Hermes environment. When necessary to answer Joseph’s request, the minimum relevant content may be processed by service providers used to operate Hermes and its configured AI model. Such processing is limited to delivering the requested feature. Google user data is not sold, publicly disclosed, or shared for advertising, and is not used by the application developer to train general-purpose AI models.

Data may also be disclosed where legally required or where necessary to protect against fraud, abuse, or security threats.

5. Storage and security

OAuth credentials and task data are kept in Joseph’s access-controlled private environment or in his own Google account. The integration uses Google’s OAuth 2.0 process and does not ask Joseph to provide his Google password to the application.

6. Retention and deletion

Task data is retained only as needed for Joseph’s requested work, continuity, verification, or records he chooses to keep. Joseph controls files, calendar events, drafts, and messages held in his Google account. He may revoke the integration’s access at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party connections. He may also request removal of locally retained integration data through the contact method below.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

The integration’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

8. Contact

Privacy or support enquiries may be submitted through the project’s GitHub Issues page.

9. Changes

Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
