Joseph’s Hermes Google Integration

This application connects Joseph Chen’s private Hermes assistant to his own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no commercial users.

What it does

  • Searches and reads Gmail messages when Joseph asks, and saves drafts for Joseph to review. It never sends email.
  • Reads and manages Joseph’s Google Calendar at his direction.
  • Reads and organises Joseph’s Google Drive files at his direction. It does not permanently delete Drive files.

How access is controlled

Access is limited to Joseph’s own account and is used only to perform tasks he requests. Joseph can revoke access at any time from his Google Account security settings.

No advertising or sale of data

The integration does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or make it available to the public.
